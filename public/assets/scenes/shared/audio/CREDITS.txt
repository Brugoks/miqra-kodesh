Miqra Kodesh — 3D environment audio

These are contemporary foley/ambience recordings used to evoke the scenes,
not historical recordings. Adaptations: mono mix, rumble/high-frequency
filtering, level matching, short edge fades; ambience loops have crossfaded
seams. Files are encoded to Ogg Vorbis. See sceneAudioManifest.js for the
mapping from each served filename to its original source file.
